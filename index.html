How the numbers work

ATP and WTA tour singles (1000s, 500s, 250s) and ATP Challenger singles are shown; men's Slams are not covered yet. A match leaves the page 3 hours after its start time (it stays in the tracker). Pinnacle posts a game handicap and a total-games line for each match. We read those lines, strip the bookmaker margin, and fit the serve strength of both players that reproduces them. The first-set total lines, each player's own games lines and the set lines (+-1.5 sets, 2.5 sets) pin how strong the serves are and how likely a third set is. From that one fitted match we price every pick below. When Pinnacle posts a real moneyline we use it for the winner chance.

What each match shows

Game handicap: the safest handicap line, spread or protection, for a player with at least a 40% chance of winning (never a heavy underdog). Set handicap: +-1.5 sets. When the feed has set lines, both sides are offered; when it has none, the model estimates the protection side (+1.5 sets) only. Total games: the expected total, plus the safest over and the safest under, within 5.5 games of the expected total. First and second set totals: games in that set (6 to 13), over or under 8.5 to 12.5; the second set is always a model estimate. Player totals: each player's own games won over or under a line. Winner: the favourite and the chance. Bet of the game: the highest-chance pick of the match at your odds floor, handicap preferred. The slider sets that odds floor.

Every line comes from the same fitted match, so a handicap such as -5.5 is chosen because the model gives the favourite a given chance of winning by 6 or more games, not just because he is a favourite.

Odds shown

Odds are fair odds: 100 divided by the chance, with no bookmaker margin. Your bookmaker pays less, typically 5 to 10% less, so check its price.

Bet of the day and special odds

Bet of the day is one pick, a double or a treble (three matches at most) with combined odds of at least 1.5, chosen once a day. Special odds of the day needs combined odds of at least 1.3 with no cap on matches. Both stay the same all day.

Accumulators and rollovers

The accumulators target odds of about 2, 3 and 5 with no cap on matches. Each match contributes its highest-chance pick that pays at least 1.18, and picks are added from the most likely downward until the combined odds reach the target. Chances multiply, so four picks at 80% each only land about 41% of the time. Rolling a bet means putting the whole return on the next pick.
